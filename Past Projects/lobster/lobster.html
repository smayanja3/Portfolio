Lobster How-To

How to eat the whole thing

First time ordering the whole dinner? Here's the order of operations. Take your time - the tide isn't going anywhere.

1
Claws First.

Twist them off and crack them open. The knuckle meat next door is the swetest bite in the whole lobster.

2
Take the tail.

Twist it away from the body and snap off the little flippers at the end.

3
Push, don't dig.

Push the tail meat out in one piece with a fork Thumb also works. Nobody's judging.

4
Don't skip the legs.

Bite down gentlyand squeeze the meat out like a straw. This is where the locals live.
